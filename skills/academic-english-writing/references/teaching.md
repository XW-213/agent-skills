# Teaching Academic Writing

Reference for teaching the skill rather than doing it for the learner: diagnosing level, sequencing knowledge points, running a single-point lesson, and checking whether the point was actually acquired.

---

## 1. Three principles

1. **Diagnose before explaining.** A learner who already writes a contestable thesis does not need the thesis lesson; he needs the evidence-interpretation lesson. Teaching the wrong point wastes the session and signals that nobody read his work.
2. **One point per session.** Two points per session is the maximum, and only if they are causally linked (e.g. topic sentence → evidence interpretation). Learners can hold one new rule; they cannot hold four.
3. **Practice must be checkable.** Every explanation ends with a task whose completion can be verified by looking at the output, not by asking "do you understand?"

---

## 2. Level diagnosis

Ask for one piece of the learner's own writing — a paragraph is enough — then run three probes.

| Probe | What to ask | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis probe | *Write one sentence stating what you want to argue.* | Whether the learner distinguishes topic from claim |
| Paragraph probe | *Which sentence in this paragraph is the main claim?* | Whether topic sentences are under conscious control |
| Evidence probe | *Why did you put this quotation here?* | Whether evidence is chosen to support a claim or to fill space |

Map the result:

| Symptom | Level | Entry point |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis is a fact, a question, or a topic label | Foundation | `templates.md` §1, contestability test |
| Thesis fine, paragraphs wander | Intermediate | `structure.md` §10, PIE |
| Structure fine, evidence uninterpreted | Intermediate-plus | `structure.md` §10 Explanation step |
| All fine, language marked by L1 errors | Advanced | `language.md` §7 inventory |
| All fine, no original angle | Advanced | Counterargument and synthesis, `templates.md` §5 |

Do not start at lesson one by default. Start at the first failing probe.

---

## 3. Knowledge dependency order

Teach in this order; each layer assumes the one above it. Skipping a layer produces the familiar situation where a learner can define *thesis* but cannot write one.

```
① 文体认知      essay vs research paper; what each is judged on
② 选题收窄      broad → narrowed → question → thesis
③ 论点          contestability, specificity, reason preview
④ 提纲          sentence outline; topic sentences as claims
⑤ 段落          PIE; unity; paragraph length
⑥ 证据整合      引用 / 改写 / 概述; sandwich structure
⑦ 引用规范      APA in-text and reference list
⑧ 语体与立场      register, hedging, reporting verbs, tense
⑨ 修改           three-pass editing
⑩ 速通／应试       rubric-driven revision under time pressure
```

If the learner's deadline is close, jump to ⑩ and teach ③⑤ only — those two carry most of the score.

## 4. Single-point lesson script

Five steps, roughly 8–12 minutes of explanation plus 10 minutes of practice.

**① 为什么重要（1 分钟）**
Show the cost, not the rule. *这一段没有那句解读，读者看到的是资料汇编，评分表上"论证"这一栏只能给一半分。*

**② 规则（2 分钟，不超过三句）**
State it as a test the learner can apply, not as a definition. *检验方法：把这段的引用句删掉，剩下的句子还能不能独立支持你的主张？如果不能，说明你在靠引用替你说话。*

**③ 正例（2 分钟）**
One strong example, annotated to show which part does the work.

**④ 反例（2 分钟）**
The same content done badly, plus a one-line diagnosis of what is missing. Contrast is what makes the rule visible; a rule alone is forgettable.

**⑤ 练习（10 分钟）**
Apply to the learner's own text, not to a textbook exercise. Textbook exercises teach the rule; own-text exercises change the writing.

## 5. Misconception inventory

These are the beliefs that survive years of English classes. Name them explicitly; a learner cannot abandon a misconception he does not know he holds.

| 学习者以为 | 实际上 |
|---|---|
| 论点越长越具体 | 论点靠范围限定变具体，不靠字数。一句 15 词的精确论点胜过长句堆砌 |
| 引用越多越显得读过书 | 引用要配解读，密度过高反而挤掉自己的论证，评分表上会判"缺少独立思考" |
| 高级词汇 = 学术水平 | 学术感来自信息密度与精确度。误用大词比用常用词扣分更重 |
| 用 *In my opinion* 显得有立场 | 第一人称标记立场是口语习惯；学术写作靠证据和限定条件表明立场 |
| 长句显得水平高 | 长句只在你控制得住从属关系时成立，否则就是 run-on |
| 不能用 *I* | APA 7 允许 *I* 和 *we*；真正要避免的是 *you* 和主观断言 |
| 结论要写新想法才有深度 | 结论引入新证据是结构性错误；深度来自对已有发现的重新解释 |
| 改写就是换同义词 | 换词不改结构 = patchwriting，属于抄袭 |
| 摘要最后随便写写 | 摘要是唯一被完整阅读的部分，五要素缺一即失分 |
| 字数够了内容就够 | 评分看论证，不看篇幅；超字数常伴随重复 |
| 老师喜欢华丽的开头 | 宏大背景（*With the development of society*）是典型失分信号 |
| 引用格式是小事 | 格式 10 分，且格式错误会被判为学术规范问题，影响观感分 |

## 6. Practice bank

Each item is short and has a checkable completion criterion. Use the learner's own material wherever possible.

**Thesis（②③）**
- 给三个题目，各写一句 thesis；判定标准：能否找到一个合理的反方立场。
- 取一篇已发表论文的摘要，遮住其结论句，自己写一句 thesis，再对照。
- 把自己的 thesis 改写成"虽然 X，但是 Y，因为 Z"结构。

**Outline（④）**
- 抽出自己文章的每个 topic sentence，单独列成清单；检查标准：这十条能不能连成一段完整的论证。
- 给一个宽泛题目，写三个互不重叠的分论点。

**Paragraph（⑤）**
- 找一段自己写的，标出 P / I / E / L 四个成分；缺哪个补哪个。
- 把一段 14 句的长段拆成两段，每段写一个新 topic sentence。

**Evidence（⑥）**
- 找三处引用，每处后面补一句 *This suggests that...*；检查标准：补出来的句子不能是原文的重复。
- 把一段直接引用改成改写，然后对照原文，确认没有连续六词相同。

**Citation（⑦）**
- 给五条文内引用，写出对应的参考文献条目。
- 制造三个格式错误（缺页码、作者名颠倒、期刊名未斜体），让学习者找出来。

**Language（⑧）**
- 一段文字里删除所有 *very / really / a lot of*，用具体信息替换；检查标准：删完后句子没有变空。
- 把五个断言句改成适当的 hedge 强度，并说明依据。

**Editing（⑨）**
- 只读每段首句，判断全文论证是否成立；写出三条结构问题。
- 倒读一遍，标出所有主谓一致和冠词错误。

## 7. Teaching register

- Explain in Chinese, exemplify in English. The learner's confusion is usually conceptual, not lexical; switching the explanation into English adds a second problem on top of the first.
- Name the error type once, then stop naming it. Repetition of the label without new information reads as scolding.
- When the learner's sentence is wrong but defensible, say so and give the condition under which it would work. Binary verdicts teach less than conditional ones.
- When the learner improves, point at the specific sentence that improved. Generic praise is not information.
- Never do the exercise for him. If he is stuck, give the first clause and let him finish.
